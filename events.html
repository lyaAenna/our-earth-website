Student Projects

*Initiatives by students at Sunway University*

1. Increasing Energy Efficiency

The university relies on a mix of renewable and non-renewable energy, primarily sourced through the national grid via Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB), which utilizes both thermal and hydroelectric power. While the campus remains largely dependent on grid electricity, current sustainability efforts center on boosting energy efficiency by cutting down on waste and systematically upgrading to energy-efficient appliances.

2. Rainwater Harvesting

Sourcing treated water indirectly adds to greenhouse gas emissions and places further strain on limited freshwater resources. To address this, a rainwater harvesting system was launched in 2017 to capture water for non-potable uses, including grounds cleaning and landscape irrigation. This initiative successfully saved 8.7 m³ of water in 2020 alone.

3. Transportation and Commuting

As the campus population grows each year, sustainable mobility remains a top priority. The university continues to invest in pedestrian-friendly walkways and facilities, reducing reliance on motor vehicles and creating a fully walkable campus environment.

*Initiatives by students at Universiti Malaysia Sabah*

1. EcoCampus Awareness Campaigns

Hosting targeted educational campaigns to build environmental awareness across campus and empower students and staff to adopt sustainable daily habits.

2. Community Engagement Programs

Expanding environmental initiatives into surrounding neighborhoods by collaborating with local communities through outreach campaigns and sustainability education.

3. Recycling and Waste Management Initiatives

Establishing comprehensive recycling initiatives and encouraging effective waste segregation to minimize the university's overall environmental footprint.
